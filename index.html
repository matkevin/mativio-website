Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This website is run by Mativio, a family-owned residential property business in Bengaluru, Karnataka ("we", "us"). This notice explains what personal data we collect through this website, why we collect it, and the choices and rights you have. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025.

1. What we collect

  • Details you give us in our forms: your name, mobile number, email address (optional), the home you're interested in, your preferred viewing day, time and type (in person or video call), the locality, size, budget and move-in month you're looking for, and any message you write.
  • Basic technical data: when you visit, the services that run this website automatically record standard information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and working.
  • Your preferences on your device: your light or dark theme choice and your grid or list view are saved only in your own browser. They are not sent to us.

We do not ask for identity documents, bank details or payment information on this website.

2. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange an in-person or video-call viewing.
  • To tell you when a home you asked about, or one that matches your requirements, becomes available.
  • To keep the website secure and fix problems.

We use your details only for these purposes, and only with the consent you give when you submit a form. We do not sell your data, and we do not send you marketing messages unrelated to your request.

3. Who can see it

Your details are seen only by the members of our family who manage the properties. We use trusted service providers to host this website and to store form submissions on our behalf. They may only use your data to provide that service to us, and they may store it on servers in India or in other countries.

Some features load content from other services, which your browser connects to directly: web fonts, the 360° tour viewer, and, only if you choose to use them, video tours (YouTube), maps (Google Maps) and WhatsApp.

We may also share data where the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and alert sign-ups for up to 12 months after our last contact with you, then delete them. If you become a tenant, your details become part of the tenancy records, which we keep for as long as the law requires.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • delete it;
  • stop using it, by withdrawing your consent, for example to stop availability alerts. Withdrawing is as easy as giving consent: reply "STOP" to any message from us, or contact us;
  • let a person you nominate exercise these rights for you if you are unable to.

To make a request or raise a complaint, contact us using the details below. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. We will respond as soon as we can, and in any case within 90 days. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

6. Security

The website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and only the people who manage our properties can access form submissions. If a breach affecting your personal data ever occurs, we will tell you without delay, explain what happened and what we're doing about it, and inform the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

7. Children

This website is meant for adults looking to rent a home. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
